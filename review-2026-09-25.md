## Verdict

The rigour is rare: every lab runs real equations, claims are tagged, and the "what would overturn it"
method now covers every idea. Phases A–C fixed the structure (home, tours, threads, deep links, predict-first).

What's left is different in kind:
1. The app **shows** well but never **checks what stuck** — engagement at the start of a lab, nothing at the end.
2. The content is weighted to relativity and cosmology; **quantum time, time travel, the evidence base and the
   human side of time are missing**.
3. **Colour carries too many meanings**, which undercuts the tagging method the app is built on.

## 1. Making it more educational

## 2. Knowledge content

### 2.1 Accuracy fix (do first)

`src/labs2.js` (Entropy box next-question): *"If the laws don't care about direction…"* — too strong.
The weak interaction violates time-reversal symmetry: inferred from CP violation in neutral kaons
(Christenson, Cronin, Fitch & Turlay 1964 ☐), observed directly by CPLEAR (1998 ☐) and in B mesons by BaBar
(2012 ☐). It is widely held to be too small and of the wrong kind to explain the thermodynamic arrow.

- **Wording:** "Almost all the laws run the same both ways — the one known exception is far too small to explain
  the arrow. So why did the universe start out so ordered?"
- **Atlas:** new idea on H3, "The weak force knows past from future" (1964), tag ESTABLISHED (the violation) /
  CONTESTED-to-no for relevance to the arrow; predicts/overturned-by in `data3.js`.
- Check other on-screen text for the same claim (Guide, tour wording, H3 text).

## 4. Flow and new content

1. **Concept map** — a second Home view drawing dependencies (simultaneity → block universe → no 'now' → H4;
   entropy → arrow → Janus → H3). Tours are paths; the map shows where you are and what you've missed. The
   `THREADS` data is half of it already.
2. **Capstone** — tours end with "Now answer H5 yourself": the hypothesis coach walks the five hurdles as
   questions, then compares with Tegmark. The tour ends in something the learner produces.
3. **Tour 3: "Is time travel possible?"** — Twin paradox → 1 g voyage → River → Gödel → wormholes (new cards)
   → verdict. The most common door into the subject.
4. **Cosmic timeline as the Cosmos spine** (already planned) — each hole and lab placed at its moment in
   cosmic history, used as navigation, not a standalone lab.
5. **Reasons to return** — "New since your last visit" strip on Home (progress already tracks visits); a
   question of the month tied to a recent paper (also useful for LinkedIn posts via deep links).
