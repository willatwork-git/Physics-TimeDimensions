## Dimensionality of spacetime
- **C** (overall conclusion; component maths E) Tegmark, M. (1997). "On the dimensionality of spacetime." *Classical and Quantum Gravity* 14, L69. arXiv:gr-qc/9702052. https://www.tegmark.org/dimensions.html — The (n, m) diagram; core of Panel 1. ☐ verify region boundaries (esp. n=1 "tachyons only").
- **E** Ehrenfest, P. (1917). On stability of orbits/atoms in n space dimensions. — Basis for "Unstable" region. ☐ exact title.
- **E** (conditional maths) Craig, W. & Weinstein, S. (2009). "On determinism and well-posedness in multiple time dimensions." *Proc. R. Soc. A* 465(2110), 3023. arXiv:0812.0210 ✓ verified. — Ill-posed for generic data; non-local constraint gives well-posedness from a full mixed-signature surface; data on lower-dimensional slices (t=s=0) can remain non-unique. Used in I.3, I.4.
- **E** 2+1 gravity has no local degrees of freedom (Deser, Jackiw, 't Hooft 1984, *Annals of Physics*). ☐

- **E** Dorling, J. (1970). "The dimensionality of time." *Am. J. Phys.* 38, 539. — With m ≥ 2, particles can decay into heavier products (cited by Tegmark). ☐
