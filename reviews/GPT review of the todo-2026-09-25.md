## Copy and accuracy fixes for Claude

- `src/deeptime.js:147` and `src/stick.js:146`: “about 40% across” is consistent with the chosen logarithmic axis, but “as many powers of ten” on each side is false. There are about 61 orders of magnitude from Planck time to today and about 90 from today to the plotted far-future endpoint. Suggested copy: “Today sits roughly 40% across this chart: about 61 powers of ten lie behind it and about 90 ahead. That is a position on a scale with chosen endpoints, not a fraction of the universe's life.”
- `src/deeptime.js:240,301-304` and `src/stick.js:95-96`: saying Earth does not really get energy, or treating “the Sun gives Earth energy” as the misconception, goes too far. Earth receives a continuous energy flow and radiates nearly the same amount back; the useful distinction is **energy received versus net energy retained**, and the low-entropy character of incoming sunlight. Make that distinction explicit in the prediction, Common trap, and explanation.
- `src/deeptime.js:48,77,100-105` and `src/stick.js:143-144`: the final flash is depicted as a definite outcome. Qualify it as the prediction of the simplified evaporation model; the final stage is beyond that model's reliable range. “Known stellar black holes” is also safer than “real ones” when saying they currently absorb more radiation than they emit.
- `src/story.js:28,137,142,182`: “entropy only rises” reads as an exceptionless rule for every part of every panel. Local entropy can fall while total entropy rises. Label the gauges as a schematic of the **overall** arrow in this cosmic story, rather than a universal rule for each object.
- `src/story.js:124-125`: “dark energy stretches space” credits ordinary cosmic expansion and redshift to dark energy. Space was expanding before dark energy dominated. Suggested lead: “All the while, space expands. Dark energy appears to make that expansion accelerate.”
- `src/stick.js:27-28`: the letter/postmark analogy says observers disagree about when light was “sent.” They agree on the emission event; the frame-dependent issue is assigning a time to a distant event or saying what is happening on Mars “now.” Rephrase that limit.
- `src/stick.js:133-134` and `src/quantum.js:30,114`: the no-backwards-signal conclusion is strong. The categorical statement that a photon **never** had a definite path is interpretation-dependent. Keep the “What it means” section tagged contested and phrase the quiz answer as “the experiment does not require a definite prior path.”
- `src/stick.js:131-132`: “with two times, the laws lose their power to predict” is broader than the Two Films demonstration. Say “with the two-time equation shown, the same complete starting data do not determine one future.”
- `src/data3.js:42`: Moreva's entangled-photon experimental illustration was published in **2014**, as correctly stated in `src/quantum.js:193` and its source line. The paper was received in 2013.
- `src/data3.js:31-35`: Wheeler–DeWitt and Dorling entries make model-specific results sound universal (“any quantum description,” “any world with two or more time dimensions”). Qualify the framework or assumptions. These are first-pass bench scores, so keep their reasons open to challenge.
